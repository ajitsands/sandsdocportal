2.0 Accounts & Financial Opening Balance Cutover Strategy
Clean Accounting Cutover SOP

Enterprise ERP implementations strictly avoid migrating fragmented 5-year line-item journal entries into a new live General Ledger, as un-reconciled legacy adjustments inevitably pollute the new double-entry balance sheet. Instead, the Popular ERP Financial Cutover SOP implements a clean, certified Opening Balance Initialization:

1. GL Opening Balances
Audited Trial Balance as of cutover date initializes the new 5-group Chart of Accounts (COA) with balanced debits and credits.
2. Customer AR Opening Balances
Verified customer-by-customer outstanding balances with aging buckets (30/60/90+ days) and pending debit memos.
3. Vendor AP Opening Balances
Verified supplier-wise payables with invoice-level reference matching and outstanding PDC commitments.
4. Inventory Valuation Opening
Physical stock count on cutover eve initialized into each branch warehouse with weighted average landed unit cost.
Audit Search Archive: All historical 5-year sales, purchase vouchers, and receipts will remain fully searchable via a dedicated Historical Query Viewer and File Store for statutory/VAT audits without compromising live financial posting.
