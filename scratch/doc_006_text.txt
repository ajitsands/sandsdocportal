PREFACE
Efficient administration and resource management are essential for maintaining 
smooth business operations, ensuring compliance, and maximizing organizational 
productivity. This document provides a comprehensive overview of the ERP 
Administration Modules designed to centralize and automate the management of 
facilities, assets, vehicles, lockers, and organizational documents within a unified 
platform.
The modules described in this document enable organizations to streamline facility 
operations, optimize asset utilization, improve fleet management, strengthen 
document control, and enhance operational visibility through real-time tracking 
and reporting. By integrating administrative processes into a single system, 
organizations can reduce manual effort, improve accountability, ensure regulatory 
compliance, and support informed decision-making.
This document serves as a reference guide for management, administrators, 
facility teams, asset custodians, fleet managers, and implementation stakeholders 
involved in configuring, operating, and maintaining the ERP system. The workflows, 
procedures, and best practices outlined herein are intended to support efficient 
administration, effective resource utilization, and sustainable organizational 
growth.
