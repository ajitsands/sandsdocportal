PREFACE
Human Resources are among the most valuable assets of any organization, and 
effective workforce management is essential for achieving operational excellence 
and sustainable growth. The HR Management Module is designed to provide a 
centralized and integrated platform for managing the complete employee lifecycle, 
from employee onboarding and record management to payroll processing, benefits 
administration, leave management, and employee self-service functions.
This module enables organizations to maintain accurate employee information, 
automate salary processing, generate salary slips, manage employee benefits and 
entitlements, monitor attendance and leave records, and ensure compliance with 
organizational policies and statutory requirements. The Employee Self-Service 
(ESS) Portal further empowers employees by providing secure access to personal 
information, leave applications, salary slips, attendance records, requests, and 
other HR-related services, reducing administrative workload and improving 
employee engagement.
By integrating HR operations into a unified ERP environment, organizations can 
streamline workforce management, enhance transparency, improve productivity, 
and support informed decision-making through real-time reporting and analytics. 
This document serves as a guide for HR professionals, administrators, managers, 
employees, and implementation teams involved in configuring, managing, and 
utilizing the HR Management Module effectively across the organization.

1. 1.HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGEMENT 
1.1. Objective
The Human Resource Management System (HRMS) is designed to establish a centralized, efficient, 
and standardized platform for managing the Popular Auto Spare Parts company’s workforce across all 
branches and operational locations. As a spare parts trading company operates through multiple sales 
outlets, warehouses, distribution centers, logistics units, and administrative offices, effective workforce 
management becomes a critical factor in ensuring operational efficiency, customer service excellence, 
and sustainable business growth.
The proposed HRMS aims to automate and streamline all employee-related processes, eliminate manual 
administrative activities, ensure regulatory compliance, improve workforce productivity, and provide 
management with real-time access to human capital information for informed decision-making.
1.2. Strategic Objectives
1.2.1  Centralized Employee Information Management
To establish a single source of truth for all employee-related information across the organization 
by maintaining comprehensive employee records, employment history, qualifications, certifications, 
contracts, payroll information, and statutory documents in a centralized database accessible according 
to authorized roles and responsibilities.
This ensures consistency, accuracy, and availability of employee information across all branches while 
reducing dependency on manual records and spreadsheets.
1.2.2  Workforce Planning and Resource Optimization
To support management in planning, allocating, and monitoring human resources effectively across 
branches, warehouses, outlets, and support departments.
The system enables management to identify staffing requirements, analyze workforce utilization, 
forecast future manpower needs, and ensure that each business unit is adequately resourced to support 
operational objectives.
1.2.3  Streamlined Recruitment and Talent Acquisition
To facilitate a structured and transparent recruitment process that enables the organization to attract, 
evaluate, and onboard qualified candidates efficiently.
The system supports manpower requisition approvals, vacancy management, candidate tracking, 
evaluation processes, and onboarding activities, thereby reducing recruitment cycle times and improving 
hiring quality.
1.2.4  Efficient Employee Onboarding and Integration
To provide a standardized onboarding process that ensures newly recruited employees are properly 
integrated into the organization through automated employee setup, department assignment, asset 
allocation, user account creation, policy acknowledgement, and training assignments.

2. Organization Structure Management
Organization Structure Management module forms the core foundation of the entire HRMS and ERP 
system, because every operational process—HR, payroll, sales approvals, procurement limits, warehouse 
operations, and financial authorizations—depends on it.
The company is distributed across:
 • Multiple countries 
 • Multiple branches per country 
 • Multiple warehouses and outlets 
 • Functional departments 
This module ensures that the entire organization operates under a single standardized digital hierarchy, 
while still supporting local flexibility per branch or country.
